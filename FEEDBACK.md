# Feedback — VUSM Student Skills Pilot

After you've used each skill for a session or two (or whenever you have a
moment), please share your experience. The feedback shapes what stays,
what changes, and whether to expand the pilot.

## REDCap survey

**Survey link:** **https://redcap.vumc.org/surveys/?s=JYXEDK4R8Y3F9AR8**

The survey takes ~5 minutes. It's anonymous unless you choose to include
your name. The first question asks **which skill** you're giving feedback
about (Student Coach or Student Tutor) and tailors the rest accordingly —
so submit it **once per skill** if you have feedback on both.

You can also reach this form from inside either skill: just say
*"feedback"* or *"report a problem"* and the skill will draft a
pre-filled survey link for you.

## What we're hoping to learn

- **Setup.** Was installing both skills easy? Any friction?
- **Student Coach.** Was the study planning genuinely useful — did it
  help you know what to study, how topics build, and how to pace it?
- **Student Tutor.** The tutor withholds answers on purpose — it hints,
  asks you to try first, checks your reasoning before confirming. Did
  that pacing help your learning, or just frustrate you? Was the
  difficulty level right?
- **What would make them more valuable.** What's missing? What would you
  use them for if you trusted them more?

The single most useful thing you can give us: **specific examples of
things a skill got wrong** about clinical content or the VUSM curriculum.
We can fix those.

## What to do if a skill says something wrong

The survey has a "safety/accuracy concern" question — use it if a skill
states an incorrect clinical fact, a broken citation, or misleading
study guidance. Include the question you asked and a paraphrase of what
the skill said.

For anything urgent (e.g., advice that could affect a patient-care
decision), email Shane directly rather than waiting for the survey.

## How to opt out of the pilot

Either reply to Shane's onboarding email asking to wind down, or email
*"please revoke my student-skills key"*. Shane revokes your API key
(≤60 seconds) and removes you from the roster — one key covers both
skills, so both stop working immediately. You can delete the skills from
Settings → Skills whenever you like.
